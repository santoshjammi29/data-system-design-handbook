## 1. Four Pillars of Data Mesh
1. **Domain Ownership**: Business domains (Billing, Logistics, Customer) own and operate their data pipelines.
2. **Data as a Product**: Datasets treat consumers as customers, providing documented schemas, SLOs, and quality contracts.
3. **Self-Serve Platform**: Infrastructure teams provide self-service tooling (IaC, CI/CD, compute provisioning).
4. **Federated Governance**: Global standards (security, compliance, taxonomy) enforced programmatically across autonomous domains.
